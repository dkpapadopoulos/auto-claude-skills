Slotwise 3.4.1 release notes

Fixes
- Reminder emails sent before 07:00 local time were dated the previous day. Reminders now use the clinic's timezone from Settings > Clinic.
- The booking widget showed "No availability" for providers whose first slot of the day was a buffer. Buffers are now skipped when computing the first slot.
- CSV export of appointments dropped the last row when the file ended without a newline.

Known issue
- Google Calendar sync can lag by up to 10 minutes after a cancellation. A fix is scheduled for 3.4.2.

Upgrade
No migration is needed. Restart the service after deploying.
