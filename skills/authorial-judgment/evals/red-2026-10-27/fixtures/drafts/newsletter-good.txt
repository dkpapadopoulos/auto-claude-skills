Issue 41: I deleted my task manager

In March I had three task managers running at once. Things for the laptop, Todoist on the phone because Things doesn't do Android, and a paper notebook for the stuff I actually did. The notebook was winning, and it had been winning for about a year before I admitted it.

The pitch for a task manager is that it holds what your head can't. That's true for about forty tasks. Past that it stops being a memory and becomes a backlog, and a backlog is a different object: it accumulates, it doesn't get done, and it makes you feel worse every time you open it. Mine had 212 items on the day I exported it. I had completed eleven of them in the previous month.

So I deleted both apps. Not archived. Deleted, with the 212 items, on 9 March. The notebook holds the week. Anything that doesn't make it onto this week's page either comes back on its own, because it matters, or doesn't, because it didn't.

Two things happened that I didn't predict. The first is that nothing fell through. Nobody emailed to ask where the thing was. The second is that I started finishing the week's page, which I had never once done with the apps.

I'm not telling you to delete yours. I'm telling you to count the items and count the ones you finished last month. If the ratio looks like mine, the app isn't holding your tasks. It's holding your guilt.
