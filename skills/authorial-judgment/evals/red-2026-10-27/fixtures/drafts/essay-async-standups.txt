Why Your Standup Should Be Async

Daily standups are a staple of modern software teams. It's worth noting that they originated in the agile movement as a lightweight way to keep everyone aligned. But in today's distributed world, the synchronous standup has become a relic.

Let's unpack why. First, time zones make it almost impossible to find a slot that works for everyone. Second, standups interrupt deep work. Third, most of what gets said could be a message.

An async standup, by contrast, is like a river that keeps flowing: everyone adds their update when it suits them, and the stream of information never stops. Teams that adopt async standups report higher satisfaction and better focus.

Of course, some will say that face time matters. And it does. But that's what retros and one-on-ones are for.

In conclusion, the synchronous standup is a habit, not a necessity. Teams that let go of it will find themselves more focused, more flexible, and more productive. The future of work is async, and the standup is just the beginning.
